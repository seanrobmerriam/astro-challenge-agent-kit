# Template Differentiation Review

## Purpose

Prevent visual and structural convergence across the collection.

## Dimensions

Customer/use case, information architecture, page inventory, layout grammar, density, typography, component composition, navigation, content model, imagery, interaction, and shape/elevation language.

## Procedure

1. Compare every template pair using the 0–2 scoring model in `instructions.md`.
2. Capture route screenshots or structured descriptions for home, one interior page, and one state-heavy page.
3. Identify repeated hero structures, card grids, navigation shells, pricing blocks, footers, table treatments, and typography combinations.
4. Separate legitimate core-system consistency from accidental product sameness.
5. For each failing pair, redesign the weaker product in at least three high-impact dimensions.
6. Re-score after changes and record evidence.
7. Run a copy review to ensure headlines, section orders, labels, and demonstration data are not formulaically repeated.

## Required output

Update `docs/differentiation-matrix.md` with:

- Pairwise scores.
- Evidence.
- Identified collisions.
- Remediation performed.
- Final pass/fail status.

## Validation

Every pair must score at least 16/24 and must be clearly distinct in customer/use case plus four additional dimensions.
